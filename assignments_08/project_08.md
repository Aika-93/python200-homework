The Azure Pricing Calculator is useful because it helps estimate costs before creating resources.

I compared Scenario A and Scenario B.

Scenario A (small VM) costs about $1.66 per month and about $19.97 per year.
Scenario B (GPU VM, database, and storage) is much more expensive, about $2,233 per month and around $26,805 per year.

I noticed that the cost increases a lot when using more powerful resources and always-on workloads. This shows that in cloud computing you pay based on usage, and it is important to choose resources carefully.
